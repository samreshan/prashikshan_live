About the Program

Why Branding Matters

From Mesopotamian markings in 7600 BC to the disciplined brand management pioneered by P&G in the 20th century, branding has always been about building trust at scale.

Today, the same story plays out closer to home. Think of Barmajiya ko Peda or Indra Chowk ko Lassi—beloved names now crowded by imitators because the original creators did not protect their identity early enough.

Strong brands are the difference between being a category leader and getting lost in the noise. The Branding Playbook equips you with the thinking, tools, and frameworks to create enduring brand value.

Whether you lead a startup, manage marketing for a growing business, or guide brand strategy for clients, this program helps you move beyond campaigns and into long-term brand stewardship.
