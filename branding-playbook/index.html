Why this playbook

Build trust that stays chosen

Branding has always been a promise of consistency. From Mesopotamian markings to modern brand management, the brands that win are the ones people can trust—even when imitators enter the market.

Think of Barmajiya ko Peda or Indra Chowk ko Lassi—beloved experiences now crowded by sound-alike competitors because the originals didn't protect their identity soon enough. In an era of infinite choice, memorability is no longer a bonus; it is survival.

The Branding Playbook equips you with systems thinking, creative confidence, and the discipline to steward brands over time. We go beyond campaign bursts to build long-term equity that wins preference, pricing power, and advocacy.

Grounded in the American Marketing Association's timeless definition of a brand, you will learn how to infuse identity with meaning, create memorable experiences, and safeguard your reputation while you scale.

Who should join: This program is designed for start-up founders, brand & marketing managers, creative leaders, digital growth practitioners, faculty teaching marketing, consultants, and anyone who influences how customers experience their organization. We welcome cross-functional teams—attending with product, sales, or customer success peers unlocks richer implementation back at work.

Program modules

A guided journey from insight to execution

Each module blends case studies, Nepal-centric context, collaborative labs, and reflective assignments.

  1. 01

    Understanding brand & branding

    Decode the language of brands, how meaning is created, and what it takes to stay memorable in crowded markets.

  2. 02

    Brand management process

    Build a disciplined workflow—from discovery to continuous improvement—that keeps teams aligned.

  3. 03

    Diagnosis

    Identify the gap between promise and perception using research frameworks, brand audits, and sentiment analysis.

  4. 04

    Targeting & positioning

    Choose the right audience, frame the competition, and articulate a unique value proposition with clarity.

  5. 05

    Brand elements & objectives

    Design names, symbols, and experiences that reinforce identity while serving measurable business goals.

  6. 06

    Brand architecture & portfolio

    Structure offerings, sub-brands, and partnerships so each adds value without diluting your core promise.

  7. 07

    Execution lab

    Translate strategy into campaigns, rituals, and governance—complete with launch plans and measurement dashboards.
