About National Cyber Olympiad Nepal

Promoting ICT learning and innovation among students in Nepal.

The National Cyber Olympiad Nepal (NCO) is Nepal's largest inter-school ICT quiz competition. Since its inception in 2014, it has provided a stage for young talents to demonstrate their skills in Information Technology.

Participants engage in diverse topics, including ICT curriculum, general ICT knowledge, logical reasoning, and current trends in technology.

NCO fosters collaboration, learning, and innovation among students, helping them prepare for a tech-driven future.

Our Objectives

What we aim to achieve through NCO:

Encourage Innovation

A platform for students to display creativity and ICT skills.

Spread IT Knowledge

Increase awareness and understanding of ICT among school students.

Foster Collaboration

Encourage unity and partnerships among diverse schools.

About

The National Cyber Olympiad is an annual ICT competition aimed at promoting technology learning among school students across Nepal.
