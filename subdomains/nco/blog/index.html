National Cyber Olympiad - Semifinals

Sep 10, 2016. | By: Admin

10 teams out of 50 teams were able to get into the semifinals of Third National Cyber Olympiad 2073. SOS School A, Sainik Awashiya Mahavidhyala, Gyanodaya School, Valley View School, Naulo Jyoti School A, Bijeshwori Gyan Mandir, Wiz Kidz School A, Lab School A, Lyceum Model School, Reliance Int. School B were the teams to compete with eachother in semifinals held today at Liberty College, Anamnagar.

Among the 10 teams, 4 were able to reach the finals. The four teams are Reliance International Academy, Sainik Awashiya Mahavidhyala, SOS Hermann Gmeiner School and Wiz Kids Academy. They will be facing each other in finals.

National Cyber Olympiad - Qualifying round Day 2

Sep 7, 2016. | By: Admin

Second day of qualifyin round of National Cyber Olympiad 2073 was successfully held at Bagmati Model College, Sukedhara on 2016-09-7. Total 25 teams played the game in 5 groups. Bijeshwori Gyan Mandir, Team A from Wiz Kidz School, Team A from LAB School, Lyceum Model School, Group B from Reliance Int. School won from Group F, G, H, I, J respectively. They will be participating again in semi finals along with the winner from Qualifying round Day 1.

Winners List with final points.

Round Team Points
F Bijeshwori Gyan Mandir 215
G Wiz Kidz School A 135
H LAB School A 185
I Lyceum Model School 135
J Reliance Int. School B 135

National Cyber Olymipad - Qualifying round Day 1

Sep 5, 2016. | By: Admin

First qualifying round of National Cyber Olympiad 2073, organized by Brushup Online was held at Liberty College on 2016-09-05. Total 24 teams out of 25 teams played the game. One team from Modern Boarding English School was absent during the game so they were directly disqualified from the game. Total of 5 rounds were played today. In each round, 5 teams played against each other. Group A from SOS Hermann Gmeiner School, Sainik Awashiya Mahavidhyala, Gyanodaya School, Valley View School, Group A from Naulo Jyoti School won from Round A, B, C, D, E respectively. They will be participating again in semi finals.

Winners List with final points.

Round   Team Points
A   SOS School A 100
B   Sainik Awashiya Mahavidhyala 170
C   Gyanodaya School 160
D   Valley View School 110
E   Naulo Jyoti School A 110

National Cyber Olymipad - 3rd Inter School ICT Quiz 2073 to be held from Bhadra 14

Jul 1, 2016. | By: Admin

Brush Up Online is all set to organize the third National ICT Quiz this year from Bhadra 14th under National Cyber Olympiad.

This event focuses on school level students particularly across the country. Last year, 46 schools’ 49 team participated in this mega event where Nightingale School became the champion.

Organizer Brush Up online is expanding this event out of valley from this year, as president Sushil Upreti said in press briefing. He also noted that there will be individual competition for students apart from team quiz.

Details and resource materials about the event can be found in this website.
